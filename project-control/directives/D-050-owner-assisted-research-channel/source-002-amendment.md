  notice. New/open owner-addressed requests must be surfaced (a) in the build session's
  owner-visible turn output at every owner-facing seam (wave summaries, landings, handoff

  when new requests are appended — batched, one line, respecting the notification-worthiness
  bar — and (c) by the companion session whenever the owner converses with it. The
  loop-monitoring quiet rule is narrowed accordingly: owner-addressed research requests are
  never silent.
- Unchanged: the non-blocking rule (the loop still never waits), leads-not-sources (R002),
  routing (R003), and every capture/verification discipline.
