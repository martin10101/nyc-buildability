## Owner message (verbatim)

Transcript timestamp 2026-10-09T19:28:18.815Z. 6007 characters; the digest is of the raw text.

> Apply this below for the pdf as well
> PDF production workflow
>
> Apply the status labels Verified, Provisional, Illustrative, Conditional, Pending verification, and Unresolved consistently throughout the PDF. Use these labels to distinguish confirmed outputs, provisional or illustrative material, conditional results, items awaiting verification, and matters that cannot yet support a conclusion.
>
> 1. Establish the required outputs
>
> Prepare the main results first, alongside the site or building drawing and a compact floor schedule, so the outcome is understandable at a glance.
>
> Identify all required deliverables, including:
>
> - Site and building drawings
> - Floor schedules
> - Development-option comparisons
> - Required report sections
> - Supporting calculations and evidence references
>
> Assign one status label to every output:
>
> - Verified: supported by completed checks and evidence
> - Provisional: prepared but subject to confirmation or revision
> - Illustrative: included to demonstrate a possible arrangement or test case
> - Conditional: dependent on stated assumptions or future checks
> - Pending verification: awaiting a specific review, measurement, approval, or calculation
> - Unresolved: not capable of supporting a conclusion because necessary information or checks are unavailable
>
> Distinguish completed outputs from those awaiting verification or further review. Show the status in headings, tables, captions, schedules, and cross-references.
>
> 2. Prepare the comparison of development options
>
> Present the development options in a concise comparison table or equivalent graphic. For each option, show:
>
> - The allowed capacity
> - The capacity or arrangement achieved by the proposed building
> - The assumptions used
> - The relevant status label
> - The required comparison outputs for the final report
> - Any unresolved condition affecting interpretation
>
> Do not describe an Illustrative, Conditional, Provisional, or Pending verification option as optimal, compliant, feasible, or preferred. Mark any option affected by unresolved information as Unresolved. Ensure that the comparison distinguishes what is permitted from what has actually been demonstrated by the proposal.
>
> 3. Display unresolved conditions with the affected results
>
> Keep important unresolved conditions visible beside the results they affect. State shared conditions once, then provide expandable or referenced detail where appropriate.
>
> For every affected result, identify whether it is:
>
> - Conditional on stated assumptions
> - Pending verification
> - Subject to further review
> - Dependent on unresolved information
> - Unresolved because the available information or checks do not support a conclusion
>
> Do not hide unresolved conditions in supporting material or imply that a result is final before the necessary checks are complete.
>
> 4. Link supporting evidence through the review register
>
> Keep full formulas, legal readings, test comparisons, and review history in the existing review register. Link each relevant register entry from the corresponding result, drawing, schedule, comparison, or report section.
>
> For every evidence item, record:
>
> - The linked output
> - The source or calculation
> - The review status
> - The applicable status label
> - Any assumptions, limitations, or unresolved matters
> - The date and history of review where relevant
>
> Supporting evidence must remain traceable without overstating its significance. Evidence does not convert an unverified, provisional, illustrative, conditional, or unresolved result into a confirmed conclusion.
>
> 5. Assemble and review the PDF structure
>
> Apply the same hierarchy and status-labeling scheme throughout the full PDF. Include drawings, schedules, comparisons, concise explanatory notes, referenced detail, and all promised report sections.
>
> Before issue, confirm that:
>
> - Every required output is present or explicitly marked Pending verification or Unresolved
> - Completed content is distinguished from evidence or material awaiting resolution
> - Verification status is visible wherever it affects interpretation
> - Conditional assumptions are stated beside the affected result
> - Unresolved conditions are not concealed
> - Status labels are used consistently in headings, tables, captions, schedules, and cross-references
> - All links to supporting evidence and review-register entries work
> - The PDF does not imply that an unverified result is final, compliant, feasible, optimal, or preferred
>
> 6. Apply calculation and drawing safeguards
>
> Continue the current calculation work using actual calculated geometry for drawings. Do not substitute conceptual geometry for calculated dimensions without identifying the limitation.
>
> Label any illustrative design, test case, or provisional arrangement as Illustrative or Provisional, as applicable. The three-storey example may remain a candidate for testing, but label it Conditional, Pending verification, or Unresolved wherever appropriate.
>
> Do not describe the three-storey example, or any other unverified arrangement, as optimal, compliant, feasible, or preferred until the necessary checks are complete. Do not present any Illustrative, Provisional, Conditional, Pending verification, or Unresolved design as the preferred solution or as evidence that the proposal can be delivered.
>
> 7. Complete the final production check
>
> Before issuing the PDF, perform a final status and calculation review:
>
> - Confirm that every number has an appropriate status label
> - Recheck calculations against the actual geometry shown
> - Confirm that comparisons use the correct allowed capacity
> - Verify that assumptions are stated and traceable
> - Confirm that unresolved conditions remain visible
> - Check that required outputs and report sections are complete or clearly marked as outstanding
> - Confirm that no provisional, illustrative, conditional, pending, or unresolved material is presented as a confirmed conclusion
> - Record any remaining issue in the review register and mark the affected output Unresolved or Pending verification as appropriate
