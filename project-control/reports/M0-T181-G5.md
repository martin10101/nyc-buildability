Windows FILETIME (100 ns ticks, absolute since 1601) and POSIX /proc starttime (clock ticks since boot) are both monotonic and read on the SAME host/boot for root and candidates, so comparisons are consistent. - root_start capture (_ContainedSpawn.__call__, mrl_one_shot.py ~line 508): captured with process_start_token(self.pid) IMMEDIATELY after Popen while the worker is alive and held (pid not yet reusable), so it is the worker's real creation time — it cannot be too large (no over-exclusion).
