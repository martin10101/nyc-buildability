## Why steps 3-9 are one coupled follow-up, not completable cleanly in this pass
1. **Single-fixture coupling.** The 1.5.0 rear_yard/input_facts emission (step 4), the DB-245
   wording sweep (step 8), AND the benchmark regeneration (step 9) all converge on ONE file,
   `recorded_215_16_northern_journey.json` (regenerated by `UPDATE_JOURNEY_FIXTURE=1` through
   `test_215_16_northern_journey.py` - the repository's own generation path). No correct subset
   smaller than "all of 3-9" can leave that fixture in a consistent, test-green state. G-6 (step 1)
   and the block-frontage MEASURE (step 2) are the only pieces that touch no fixture and are
   behaviour-identical/pure, so they are the clean separation boundary.
2. **Result-deciding legal output + independent-review discipline.** Steps 3-5/8 decide the real
   benchmark's rear-yard RESULT and its emitted provenance. Per D-093 / principle 7 (producer !=
   verifier) and the owner's "finish one implementation piece at a time and obtain independent
   review", the result-deciding emission should land as its own reviewed increment before the
   wording sweep layers on; big-banging 3-9 maximises the chance a single legal/wording error
   returns the whole wave to rework.
3. **Regeneration fan-out.** The planner's DECISION 2 lists ~15 benchmark-consumer test files to
   update on regen and noted it "could not fully enumerate-with-existence every one". That cascade
   (plus the DXF/SVG snapshots and the two synthetic re-derivations) is the bulk of the risk and is
   best done as one bounded, reviewed change.

## Design hand-off for steps 3-9 (verified against the R6B reference case and the schema)
- **Key legal insight that de-risks the core (confirm with the gate/research):** the real lot's
  short-dimension (b) answer is **not known** because the measurement BASIS is unresolved law
  (reference row `b-measurement-basis`: the captured ZR 12-10 text does not fix street-line vs
  centreline vs lot-line). So `rear_yard_checks.py` must mark the (b) check `not_known` for a REAL
  lot regardless of any measured separation - it must NOT conclude "not a short dimension" from a
  single measured basis (centreline ~260 >= 230) when the plausible street-line/tax-map bases
  (reference: ~200.01 / ~203.13 ft, both < 230) could make it short. The made-up cases M1-M10 state
  a dimension on a GIVEN basis, so there the strict `< 230` test decides (M1 229.99 applies; M2
  230.00 and M3 230.01 not; 229.996 must never print as 230.00).
- **rear_yard block shape (schema `$defs.rear_yard`, additive/optional, binds contract_version
  1.5.0):** `checks` (one per provision) + `combined`. A `not_known` check carries NO inputs/number
  (schema oneOf). For the real benchmark: (a) `23-344(a)` **applies** with measured corner reach
  144.60 ft and angle ~89.7 deg (basis `derived`) - it waives within 100 ft of the corner only;
  (b) `23-344(b)` **not_known** (basis unresolved), no number, zr_sections `["ZR 23-344","ZR
  12-10"]`; (c)(3) `23-344(c)(3)` **not_known** (adjoining zoning lot's lot-line kind not captured).
  `combined`: state `not_known`, way `conditional`, conditions (kind `unchecked_condition`) naming
  the measurement basis, the zoning lot (Z1/Z2 not confirmed), and the neighbours' side lot lines;
  text a labelled-draft conditional reading citing ZR 23-344(b) - NEVER an unconditional "no rear
  yard" (brief rule; S13). (a) is never the only exception named (D-094-R037/S11).
