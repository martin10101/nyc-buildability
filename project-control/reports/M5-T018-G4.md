## Numbered findings

**F-1 (ADVISORY) — Render-time `boundedText` bounding of `label`/`scope_note`/`over_built_statement` is untested.**
The validator does NOT length-cap these fields (`checkNonEmptyString`/`checkNullableString` have no max), so the render-time `boundedText` (cap 600, control-char strip) is the *sole* bound. No test feeds oversized (>600) or control-char input, and the fixtures use clean short strings where `boundedText(x)===x`, so a mutant that dropped `boundedText` (rendering raw) survives. Impact is bounded: React escaping (not `boundedText`) is the injection control, so the residual risk is DOM/a11y-tree flooding from a hostile/misbehaving upstream — a hardening the producer explicitly claims but does not prove by test. **Not blocking** (defense-in-depth, low-probability trigger, `boundedText` itself is unit-tested elsewhere). Recommend a follow-up test asserting truncation/control-strip at the section.

**F-2 (ADVISORY) — Corrupted cross-field state combinations untested.**
The schema/mirror carry no per-state coupling, so `state:"computed"` with `value:null` is schema-valid; it renders `formatValue(null)="—"` with the unit suppressed (honest, no fabricated number), but no test pins this, nor the inverse (`not_computable` with a numeric value → component still shows no number because routing is by `state`). Behavior is correct; the invariant is simply unpinned. Not blocking.

**F-3 (ADVISORY) — Unknown-key coverage is asymmetric.**
S1 exercises `checkNoUnknownKeys` at the section level and at `inputs.existing_building_floor_area`, but not at the `inputs` object level itself or at `inputs.draft_zoning_floor_area_cap`. The mechanism is identical and tested at two levels, so the hole risk is low. Recommend adding one case at the `inputs`/`cap` level. Not blocking.

**F-4 (ADVISORY) — `scope_note`/DRAFT presence not pinned on the over_built and not_computable states.**
The `scope_note` node is rendered unconditionally at section level, and DRAFT rides `ComputedLine` (computed + over_built). S2 pins these only on the computed state. A regression that gated `scope_note` on state would not be caught on the other two states. Not blocking.

**F-5 (ADVISORY / modularity signal) — `scenario-contract.ts` at 576 SLOC is 24 below the 600 WARN threshold.**
Grew +155 SLOC (421→576) in this task. It is not in the modularity baseline and stays under WARN, so `--check` is clean today, but the module is close to the warning line; the next section addition should extract into `scenario-contract-checks.ts` (which was out of this task's scope, forcing the two new local primitives `checkNullableFiniteNumber`/`checkNullableObject` — generic, vocabulary-free checks — to live in `scenario-contract.ts` rather than the checks module). Disclosed by the producer. Not blocking.
