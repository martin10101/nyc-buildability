## 3. Variant analysis (same mechanism elsewhere)

- **Unverified expected-vs-observed (F3/F4)** recurs in every recovery probe: they all *record* and *shape-check*. The launch-manifest verifier therefore compares ALL twelve R559 fields in one place rather than patching individual probes.
- **Partial hashing (F7)** recurs at every `executable_identity` caller (`start_gate.py:94-109`, `cli.py:584-595`, `recovery_probes.py:73`); the legacy callers keep their key semantics (they key journals/channels), and the *dispatch* path gets the complete streaming digest.
- **Second-message / resume (F16)** recurs at seven sites; the one-shot runner refuses at its own boundary (any `extra_turns > 0`, any resume/turnover actuation) so that each site is denied by the same contract rather than seven edits.
- **Unverified constructed env (F10)** recurs at the two disclosed seams (`capability_probe._run`, `native_runtime._run`); those are not dispatch seams and stay as disclosed; the dispatch seam verifies.
- **Unproven termination (F20)** recurs on both the job-object and the `taskkill` fallback paths; the proof is placed after `terminate_all` regardless of mechanism.
- **Competing launch docs (F22)** recur in four places; two are in scope (`CONTROLLER_UPDATE_RUNBOOK.md`, `tools/agent_supervisor/README.md`); `.claude/skills/loop-start/SKILL.md` and `.claude/hooks/loop_command_interceptor.py:202` are outside the packet — recorded as an open item for the orchestrator, not silently patched.
