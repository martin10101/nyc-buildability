## 2. Findings by severity

### BLOCKING-1 — the evidence document is a silent PROJECTION: it drops 18 of the rule_evaluation contract's required fields, including every qualification the source attaches to the cap it presents

**Location:** `services/api/app/api/v1/evidence.py:292-312` (the per-trace `rule_citations` projection) and `:328-351` (the `source_coverage` / `evaluated_input` projections). Both build new dicts from a hand-picked key list rather than transporting the sub-documents.

**Measured.** I built the evidence document and the rebuilt `rule_evaluation` over identical seams and diffed the key sets. The `rule_evaluation` root has 20 keys (all 20 `required`, `additionalProperties: false`); each `evaluation_trace` has 19 keys (all 19 `required`). The evidence document represents 14 root keys and **7** trace keys.

```
DROPPED root.lot_area_source      = "spatial_intersection.pairs[].lot_area_sq_ft"
DROPPED root.lot_area_sq_ft       = 10000.0
DROPPED root.zoning_district      = "R5"
DROPPED root.rule_conflict        = null
DROPPED root.spatial_context      = {...}
DROPPED root.spatial_uncertainty  = {...}

evidence keeps per trace: ['citations','coverage_status','family','outputs','rule_id','rule_status','rule_version']
DROPPED trace.computation_steps   DROPPED trace.exceptions_applied   DROPPED trace.notes
DROPPED trace.evaluated_inputs    DROPPED trace.effective_window     DROPPED trace.rule_release
DROPPED trace.applicability_outcome  DROPPED trace.applicability_trace
DROPPED trace.input_validation    DROPPED trace.data_completeness
DROPPED trace.uncertainty         DROPPED trace.determination
```

All 17 of those field names appear **zero** times in `evidence.py` and **zero** times in `test_evidence_api.py`, and the producer report does not mention any of them. This is a silent omission, not a disclosed scope decision.

**Concrete failure scenario — on the default happy-path fixture (F01, a confident R5 lot), not a contrived edge case.** For BBL `1000010100` the evidence document presents, as the evidenced claim:

```
rule_citations[0].outputs = {"max_residential_far": 1.5, "max_residential_floor_area_sq_ft": 15000.0}
rule_citations[0].claim_verification_status = "draft"
```

and silently discards everything the source attached to that very number:

1. `trace.exceptions_applied[0]` — `{"id":"qualifying_residential_site","effect":"conditional_alternative","description":"A higher maximum residential FAR (up to 2.00 for R5/R5A/R5B per ZR 23-21) applies to zoning lots that are 'qualifying residential sites'. … when the site class is unknown or qualifying, **the result is conditional and the higher FAR may apply**."}` → the presented 15000.0 could legitimately be up to 20000.0, and the evidence view gives no hint the result is conditional.
2. `trace.exceptions_applied[1]` — a `documented_limitation`: a 0.60 equivalent-FAR per-dwelling-unit cap (ZR 23-21) that "requires per-dwelling-unit inputs this rule does not model" → a known limitation of the number, dropped.
3. `trace.notes[1]` — "exception qualifying_residential_site was applied although its condition reads input(s) not supplied: ['site_class']; the exception is applied … but **it is NOT an evidence-based determination**" → the source's own statement that this is not evidence-based, dropped from the *evidence* endpoint.
4. `trace.computation_steps` — `[{"step_id":"far","op":"identity","resolved_args":[1.5],"result":1.5,"note":"Select the standard-zoning-lot maximum residential FAR for the district (ZR 23-21 table)."},{"step_id":"floor_area","op":"multiply","resolved_args":[10000.0,1.5],"result":15000.0,"note":"Maximum residential floor area = lot area (sq ft) x FAR."}]` → the derivation of the cap. Combined with the dropped root `lot_area_sq_ft` (10000.0) and `lot_area_source`, the document contains the **product** 15000.0 but neither factor nor the provenance of either factor. An endpoint whose stated purpose is "see WHERE every number came from" drops the field literally named `lot_area_source`.
5. `trace.effective_window` — `{"effective_from":"2024-12-05","effective_to":null,"evaluated_as_of":null,"in_effect":true}` → the temporal validity of the rule version. The packet names "stale" as a gap kind; with no effective window the document cannot show staleness, and CLAUDE.md principle 3 forbids treating effective dates casually.
6. `trace.rule_release` — `{"lifecycle_status":"needs_review","deterministic_tests":"declared","independent_review":"pending","qualified_human_approval":"pending","verified_eligible":false}`. The schema describes this as the field carrying verification eligibility and G6 approval state. The module authors its own `claim_verification_status` from `coverage_status` alone while discarding the source's own statement of *why* nothing is verified. The label it produces is correct; the evidence for it is thrown away.

**Why this is BLOCKING rather than a design note.** AS-2's narrow test ("the cap and every citation text are byte-identical … no arithmetic on or rewriting of a legal value") **passes** — I verified byte equality myself, and AS-1's enumerated (a)–(d) contents are all literally present. The defect is against the packet's **hard rule**, stated in the objective and AS-2: "no summarisation that could change meaning." Presenting a cap stripped of the source's "the result is conditional and the higher FAR may apply", its documented limitation, and its "NOT an evidence-based determination" note **does** change the legal meaning of that number — it renders a qualified figure as unqualified, in the one surface built so a reviewer or expediter can audit it. It also violates the objective's and AS-5's "never silently omitted": 18 required fields are dropped with no typed gap marker naming them and no disclosure anywhere.

**Exact minimal fix I would require (NOT applied).** Transport the sub-documents whole instead of re-keying them. In `assemble_evidence_document`:

- replace the hand-picked trace dict (`:294-312`) with `{**copy.deepcopy(trace), "claim_verification_status": _verification_status(trace["coverage_status"])}` — one added server-authored key on an otherwise verbatim trace; and
- replace the hand-picked `source_coverage` / `evaluated_input` dicts (`:328-351`) with a deep copy of the rebuilt document's own root fields and its `evaluated_input` sub-document (excluding only `evaluations`, which `rule_citations` already carries, and `contract_version`, which the envelope restates), so `lot_area_sq_ft`, `lot_area_source`, `zoning_district`, `rule_conflict`, `spatial_context` and `spatial_uncertainty` travel.

Then make the test honest: assert `set(evidence_trace) - {"claim_verification_status"} == set(rule_evaluation_trace)` and the same for the root, so a future contract field cannot be dropped silently. Both changes are entirely inside `allowed_paths`; no forbidden path is involved. If the team instead *wants* a reduced view, that is a legitimate product choice — but then the omission must be declared in the document (a typed gap marker per omitted section) and in the packet, not left implicit.

### HIGH-1 — `rule_conflict` is dropped and `_gap_markers` has no branch for it, so the typed legal-ambiguity object the engine deliberately preserves for reviewers never reaches the evidence view

**Location:** `evidence.py:234-280` (`_gap_markers` reads only `coverage_status`, `needs_review`, `fail_safe`, `evaluations`, `profile["provenance"]`) and `:328-343` (`source_coverage` omits `rule_conflict`).

The schema describes `rule_conflict` as a "typed same-family rule-conflict object (competing rule IDs + each rule's effective window) when >=2 same-family rules are simultaneously in effect and independently applicable for overlapping outputs (**a legal ambiguity surfaced for professional review**)". `app/rules/integration.py:404-410` is explicit about its purpose: on a conflict, coverage becomes `professional_review_required`, `evaluations` is emptied, and "the known district and its input provenance **are PRESERVED** … so **a reviewer still sees the full provenance trail**."

**Failure scenario.** A lot whose family has two simultaneously-effective applicable rules gets an evidence document that says `evidence_completeness: "professional_review_required"` plus gaps `professional_review_required` and `not_available/evaluation_trace` — and contains **no indication of which rules compete or over what windows**. The generic markers do fire (I confirmed the coupling: a conflict forces `professional_review_required` and an empty `evaluations`), so the trail is not wholly silent; but the specific typed object the engine preserved for exactly this reviewer, plus the `zoning_district` and `lot_area_source` it deliberately kept alongside it, are all dropped. That is AS-5's "conflicting … carrying a typed reason, never silently omitted" unmet at the level of detail that makes the conflict actionable.

**Honest bound on my evidence:** I could not drive a live `rule_conflict` document through the fixture harness — it needs ≥2 same-family rules in effect, and constructing one would require editing `app/rules/**`, a forbidden path. The omission itself is certain (zero references in the module) and the code path is plain on reading; the live end-to-end reproduction is the part I could not perform. Fixing BLOCKING-1 as specified carries `rule_conflict` into `source_coverage` and resolves most of this; a gap-marker branch for a non-null `rule_conflict` would close it fully.

### MEDIUM-1 — the server-side rebuild chain is now duplicated in a third route module

`evidence.py:468-494` reproduces the fetch → substrate → `build_property_profile` → `validate_profile` → `evaluate_property` → `serialize_rule_evaluation` → `validate_rule_evaluation_document` chain, together with the connector-error mapping (`:419-437`) and the `no_match` 404 (`:452-466`). `build_property_profile` now appears in five route modules — its two owners plus `scenario.py`, `scenario_analysis.py` and `evidence.py`. This is the LOW-1 I raised on M5-T012, now a third copy: a future correction to the rebuild chain or the error mapping must be applied in three places or they drift. Forced again by `forbidden_paths`, so not a contract breach — but it has crossed from a note into a maintenance liability, and warrants a dedicated extraction task.

### MEDIUM-2 — the AS-1 test is named for a guarantee it does not assert, which is why the omission shipped

`services/api/tests/api/test_evidence_api.py:~31` — `test_as1_evidence_document_carries_the_whole_trail_verbatim` asserts byte-equality only over the keys the projection chose to keep (`profile_provenance` identities and count, `input_provenance`, `input_fingerprint`, citation identities, `coverage_status`, `outputs`). It never asserts that the trace's 19 keys or the root's 20 keys are represented, so it cannot fail when a field is dropped — yet its name claims "the whole trail". Same for the module docstring's "Every material value is transported VERBATIM" (`:24-25`): true of what is carried, silent on what is not. The fix in BLOCKING-1 should include the set-equality assertion so the name becomes true.

### LOW-1 — `_gap_markers` emits a normalised echo of `coverage_status` as a `reason`

`evidence.py:258` and `:265` and `:270` put `coverage` — which is `str(rule_evaluation.get("coverage_status","")).strip().lower()` (`:238`) — into the emitted `reason` field. In a document whose rule is verbatim transport, a `.strip().lower()`-normalised echo of a transported token is the wrong default even though it is benign today (the canonical coverage vocabulary is already lowercase, so no transformation actually occurs). Either echo `rule_evaluation["coverage_status"]` unchanged, or rename the field so it reads as a server-authored slug rather than a transported reason.

### LOW-2 — one reason value is aliased rather than deep-copied

`evidence.py:245` sets `"reason": rule_evaluation.get("fail_safe_reason")` by reference, while the same value is `copy.deepcopy`'d at `:337`. Harmless for a read-only response, but inconsistent with the deepcopy discipline the module otherwise applies uniformly; a reader cannot tell whether the inconsistency is deliberate.

### LOW-3 — the producer report does not disclose the projection at all

`project-control/reports/M5-T013-producer-report.md` contains no mention of `computation_steps`, `exceptions_applied`, `rule_conflict`, `effective_window`, `rule_release`, `spatial_uncertainty`, `lot_area_source`, `determination` or `uncertainty`, and no statement that the evidence document is a selected view of the trace. A disclosed reduction with named gap markers would have been a MEDIUM design discussion; an undisclosed one is what makes BLOCKING-1 blocking.
