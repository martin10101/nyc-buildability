## The rule chosen for note 2, and why (5 lines)

I adopted the orchestrator's suggested rule unchanged: syntactic refusal of `*`/`?`/leading-`:`/
absolute/`..`/backslash/control, plus a "plain-set-or-tracked" test for every other character.
Reason: it is COMPLETE by construction (anything outside a tiny known-safe alphabet must prove it
binds a real file) rather than a blocklist of magic characters chased one by one, so a future
odd form (a new Unicode look-alike, a brace variant) is caught without a code change; and it keeps
the one legitimate tracked bracket route working because "tracked at HEAD" is the exact property
that makes it bind. (a)+(b) are pure posix-string; only (c) reads git, reusing the byte-unchanged
`_ls_tree_entries` under `GIT_LITERAL_PATHSPECS=1`.
