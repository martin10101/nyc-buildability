# Pitfalls — a checklist from official guidance

**Unreviewed draft (ADR-007). Not the program's finding.** Each row is a discovery-aid pointer to an
official source with its link; the one-line "watch" is a draft reading tied to that source, not a
determination by this platform. DOB Buildings Bulletins are tier-3 agency interpretations (persuasive,
not the statute); a **rescinded** bulletin controls nothing; a DOB determination is **project-specific
and is never generalized** to another property; a BSA calendar number is **project-specific, never a
general rule**. No bulletin or resolution text is bulk-copied here.

Most bulletin content below was confirmed from the official index titles + nyc.gov-scoped search
snippets, not from reading the PDF bytes (the nyc.gov asset host serves the PDFs but the research fetch
tool could not extract their text; `bb_2019-009.pdf` returned 716 KB of unparsed binary). Treat every
"watch" line as an unreviewed draft and **confirm each bulletin's current (non-rescinded) status and its
cited section numbers against the post-Dec-2024 ZR before relying on it.** Bulletin PDFs:
`https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/bldgs_bulletins/bb_<YYYY>-<NNN>.pdf`; year indexes:
`https://www.nyc.gov/site/buildings/codes/building-bulletins-<YYYY>.page`.

## DOB determinations

The DOB determinations page documents only how to SUBMIT a ZRD1 (zoning) or CCD1 (code) request through
DOB NOW against a specific job; it exposes **no searchable public list** of issued determinations. None
is enumerable by topic, and **any determination, if ever retrieved, is project-specific and must not be
generalized** (SOURCE_AUTHORITY_POLICY §2 rule 4; registry §9.6). Page:
`https://www.nyc.gov/site/buildings/about/determinations.page`.

## DCP guidance (reached only indirectly — the 2025 Zoning Handbook PDF and the districts-tools pages were blocked)

The 2025 DCP Zoning Handbook PDF (`…/nyc-zoning-handbook-2025.pdf`) exceeded the fetch tool's ~10 MB
limit and nyc.gov assets 403 non-browser clients, so **no Handbook page numbers are cited** (not
invented). The districts-tools R6 page (`…/districts-tools/r6.page`) live-returns "this page has moved"
and 403s a direct GET; its new URL is **not sure**. From a search cache (not a primary read): R6 offers a
choice between Height-Factor and Quality Housing bulk, but in **contextual R6B** the Quality Housing /
contextual controls govern — low base height, overall height cap, and street-wall lineup. **Pitfall:** the
Height-Factor setback-tower envelope is not available in R6B. The R6B-specific numbers (min base 30 / max
base 45 / max bldg 55 ft) come from the ZR 23-432 snapshot (NB-06), not from DCP guidance.
