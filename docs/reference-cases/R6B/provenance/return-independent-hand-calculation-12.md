=== Q2. DEFINITION OF FLOOR AREA RATIO ===

(a) Quote and meaning. 12-10 "floor area ratio" [C]: "'Floor area ratio' is the total #floor area# on a #zoning lot#, divided by the #lot area# of that #zoning lot#. If two or more #buildings# are located on the same #zoning lot#, the #floor area ratio# is the sum of their #floor areas# divided by the #lot area#. - 34-231 (front yard) — APPLIES [C]: "In the districts indicated, no #front yard# shall be required for any #residential building#." - 34-232 (side yard) — APPLIES [C]: "In the districts indicated, no #side yard# shall be required for any #residential building#. So no side yard is required; but any voluntarily-provided open area along a side lot line must be ≥5 ft wide.
