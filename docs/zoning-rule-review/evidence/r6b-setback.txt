EVIDENCE - r6b-setback (ZR 23-433 / 23-432 / 35-632(a)) - a labelled unreviewed DRAFT (ADR-007)

LAW (captured, pinned by content digest):
- ZR 23-432 (zr-23-432): R6B row - minimum base height 30 ft, maximum base height 45 ft
  (standard residences), maximum building height 55 ft; "For portions of a building street wall
  that exceed the maximum base height, a setback shall be provided ... in accordance with Section
  23-433."
- ZR 23-433 (zr-23-433): "a setback with a depth of at least 10 feet shall be provided from any
  street wall fronting on a wide street, and a setback with a depth of at least 15 feet shall be
  provided from any street wall fronting on a narrow street. Such minimum setbacks may be modified
  as follows: ... (c) ... optional for any building wall that either is located beyond 50 feet of
  a street line, or oriented so that lines drawn perpendicular to it ... would intersect a street
  line at an angle of 65 degrees or less."
- ZR 35-632(a) (zr-35-632): under a commercial overlay the heights come from the ZR 23-432 table
  and the setback above the maximum base height follows ZR 23-433.
- ZR 11-25 (zr-11-25): R6B inherits ZR 23 and 35 through the suffix provision.

READING (reference-case overlay-reading.json row setback-above-base; two independent AI readings
agree on the same basis):
- A setback is required ONLY for the part of a building street wall that exceeds the maximum base
  height (45 ft for R6B standard residences). The benchmark three-storey 30 ft option equals the
  minimum base height and is well below 45 ft, so no setback is triggered.
- Where a setback is triggered, the depth is at least 10 ft from a wide-street wall (Northern
  Boulevard, 100 ft) and at least 15 ft from a narrow-street wall (215 Place, 60 ft). The overlay
  does not change the depths.
- The per-frontage wide/narrow class comes from the accepted D-052 classifier (narrow below 75 ft,
  wide at or above 75 ft); a frontage whose width is unresolved or unknown withholds its depth
  (never narrow by default, D-051).

WHAT IS NOT ESTABLISHED:
- ZR 23-433(a) to (d) may reduce or adjust the depths (a one-foot-for-one-foot reduction with a
  seven-foot floor; optional beyond 50 ft of a street line or at a 65-degree orientation).
- The setback geometry on the lot is not drawn (the setback line is not_established); the depth
  runs from the operative street line, which a mapped width does not fix.
