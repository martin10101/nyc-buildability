EVIDENCE - r6b-street-wall (ZR 35-631(b) / 23-431) - a labelled unreviewed DRAFT (ADR-007)

LAW (captured, pinned by content digest):
- ZR 35-63 (zr-35-63): "In Commercial Districts mapped within, or with a residential equivalent
  of R6 through R12 Districts, the street wall location of a building shall be as set forth in
  Section 35-631 ..."
- ZR 35-631(b) (zr-35-631): "For all buildings that are not subject to the provisions of
  paragraph (a) ... At least 70 percent of the aggregate width of street walls shall be located
  within eight feet of the street line and shall extend to at least the minimum base height
  specified in Sections 23-432, or the height of the building, whichever is less."
- ZR 23-431 (zr-23-431): paragraph (a) line-up rule for R6B/R7B/R8B; paragraph (b) along wide
  streets within eight feet, along narrow streets within ten feet.
- ZR 11-25 (zr-11-25): regulations applicable to a district designation apply to the suffixed
  designation unless separate provisions are listed; R6B inherits ZR 23 and 35 through it.

READING (two independent AI readings of the sealed step-P2 captures, reference-case
overlay-reading.json row street-wall-location; both agree on the same basis):
- The C2-2 overlay changes the street-wall rule from the R6B line-up (ZR 23-431(a)) to the
  percentage rule of ZR 35-631(b), reached through ZR 35-63 (for a mixed building via ZR 35-01;
  for an all-residential building via ZR 34-11 and 34-24(b)(1)): at least 70 percent of the
  aggregate street-wall width within eight feet of each street line.
- Paragraph (a) of ZR 35-631 is limited to R8-R12 districts in the Manhattan Core, so it does
  not apply to this R6B Queens lot; paragraph (b) governs.
- Without the overlay, ZR 23-431(b) sets eight feet on a wide-street frontage and ten feet on a
  narrow one (the D-051 asymmetry; narrow is not the safe side).

WHAT IS NOT ESTABLISHED:
- The exact street-wall position within paragraph (b) is conditional on whether the block has a
  prevailing street-wall frontage (ZR 12-10 / ZR 35-631 closing clause, ZR 23-431(a) line-up) and
  on the operative street line, which a mapped street width does not fix.
