Scope
-----
This note records the evidence behind broadening the r6b-rear-yard-corner-waiver register entry from
ZR 23-344 paragraph (a) alone to the whole ZR 23-344 rear-yard reading the engine now makes:
paragraph (a) the corner-area waiver (the draft DSL rule r6b_rear_yard_corner_waiver.rule.json),

Law captured and quoted (snapshots under docs/research/zr-snapshots/v1/)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
- ZR 23-344(b) (zr-23-344): "whenever a #front lot line# of a #zoning lot# coincides with the
  #street line# of the #short dimension of a block#, no #rear yard# shall be required within 100
  feet of such #street line#".
- ZR 12-10 "short dimension of a block" (zr-12-10-short-dimension-of-a-block): "shall be a #block#
  frontage where the dimension between any two #streets# bounding the #block# measures less than
  230 feet". The threshold is strict: a dimension of exactly 230.00 feet is not a short dimension.
- ZR 23-344(c)(3) (zr-23-344): "In R6 through R12 Districts, no #rear yard# shall be required where
  such #rear lot line# coincides with a #side lot line# of an adjoining #zoning lot#".
- ZR 12-10 definitions relied on: front lot line, street line, side lot line, rear lot line, block.

The two independent readings (reference case, not a program run)
---------------------------------------------------------------
docs/reference-cases/R6B/short-block-rear-yard.md and cases/short-block-rear-yard.json carry two
independent step-P7 readings of ZR 23-344(b)/(c)(3) worked by AI helpers from a sealed folder; their
agreement alone is not proof. The settled points both readings reach:
- What (b) measures and that "less than 230 feet" is strict (made-up cases M1 229.99 applies; M2
  230.00 and M3 230.01 do not).
- (b) can apply to a corner lot; more than one 23-344 modification can apply to one lot; where (b)
  and (c) overlap the captured text sets no priority (left as a conflict).
The points both readings leave not settled (unresolved law or a missing fact):
- The measurement basis for the short dimension (street line vs centreline vs lot line) is not fixed
  by the captured text.
- "within 100 feet" and the exactly-100.00-foot edge are not fixed by the captured text.
- Whether R6B counts within "R6 through R12" for (c)(3) is held by reading 2 subject to professional
  confirmation (the captured text does not enumerate the letter-suffix districts).
