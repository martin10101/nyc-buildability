=== ITEM 2 — ZR §12-10 definition of "Floor area" ===
URL opened: https://zoningresolution.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-2/12-10 (canonical HTML, curl browser UA; read raw bytes directly).
Read (UTC): 2026-10-06, ~20:20. Note: the print/PDF completeness channel entityprint/pdf/node/18523 has returned 504 before (documented in the repo capture); I used the canonical HTML channel. The whole §12-10 page is node 18523; the "floor area" term is a "General Definition".
Version shown: term "floor area" carries <time datetime="2024-12-05T12:00:00Z">12/5/2024</time> — Last Amended 12/5/2024.
Definition opening, verbatim: "'Floor area' is the sum of the gross areas of the several floors of a building or buildings, measured from the exterior faces of exterior walls or from the center lines of walls separating two buildings. In particular, floor area includes:" — the INCLUDES list has (1) "basement space, except as specifically excluded in this definition"; (2) elevator shafts/stairwells at each floor except as excluded; (3) penthouses; (4) attic with structural headroom ≥8 ft; (5) gallerias/interior balconies/mezzanines/bridges; (6) open/roofed bridges/breezeways/porches >50% perimeter enclosed; (7) any floor space used for dwelling purposes; (8) accessory buildings except accessory mechanical-equipment space; (9) accessory off-street loading berths over 200% of required; (10) space "made inaccessible"; (11) exterior balconies/open or roofed terraces where >67% of perimeter enclosed; (12) "any other floor space not specifically excluded."
EXCLUDES list ("However, the floor area of a building shall not include:"), verbatim key items:
- Cellar: "cellar space, except where such space is used for dwelling purposes. Cellar space used for retailing shall be included for the purpose of calculating requirements for accessory off-street parking spaces, accessory bicycle parking spaces and accessory off-street loading berths;"
- Bulkheads: "elevator or stair bulkheads, accessory water tanks, or cooling towers, except that such exclusions shall not apply in R2A Districts;"
- Mechanical: "floor space used for accessory mechanical equipment. Such exclusion shall also include the minimum necessary floor space to provide for necessary maintenance and access to such equipment. For the purposes of calculating floor space used for mechanical equipment, building segments on a single zoning lot may be considered to be separate buildings;"
- Accessory parking: "floor space used for accessory off-street parking spaces provided in any story:" (1) "up to 300 square feet per single- or two-family residence..."; (2) "within group parking facilities located not more than 23 feet above curb level, except where...contained within a public parking garage;"; (3) "within automated parking facilities located not more than 40 feet above curb level, except where...contained within a public parking garage;"
- Balconies/terraces: "floor space in exterior balconies or in open or roofed terraces provided that not more than 67 percent of the perimeter of such balcony or terrace is enclosed..." with parapet (≤4 ft) / railing (≤4 ft 6 in, ≥50% open above 4 ft) / safety-guard (≤10 ft, ≥90% transparent above 4 ft) criteria.
- Exterior wall thickness: "qualifying exterior wall thickness;" (a defined term; its conditions live in that term, not here).
- Energy: "floor space within a fully electrified building or an ultra low energy building, of an amount equivalent to five percent of the floor area located within such building, and exclusive of any floor space otherwise excluded from floor area;"
- Cross-reference to §23-23: "floor space in buildings containing multiple dwelling residences allocated to building amenities, corridors, refuse storage or disposal, or access to elevated ground floor dwelling units that is provided in accordance with the provisions of Section 23-23, inclusive;" and "floor space in Quality Housing buildings that was exempted pursuant the Quality Housing Program, as such program existed prior to December 5, 2024."
Laundry / storage rooms: NOT named in the §12-10 "floor area" definition. Those are covered indirectly via the §23-23 cross-reference exclusion above (amenities under §23-231 can include laundry/storage rooms). State this as the only way the term reaches §12-10.
Amendment date shown: 12/5/2024.
Matches the reviewer's summary: yes for the enumerated includes/excludes (cellar, bulkheads, mechanical, parking, balconies/terraces, qualifying wall thickness, energy). Difference: §12-10 does not itself name "laundry or storage rooms" — only via §23-23.
